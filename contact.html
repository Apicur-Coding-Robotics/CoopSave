Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a member?

Visit the Members page and fill out the registration form with your details. An administrator will review your application.

How long does it take to process a loan application?

Most loan applications are processed within 3-5 business days. You'll receive a notification once your application has been reviewed.

What is the maximum loan amount I can apply for?

The maximum loan amount depends on your savings balance and membership history. Contact us for specific details.

Is my personal data secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard security measures to protect all member data. Your information is encrypted and stored securely.

Can I withdraw my savings anytime?

Withdrawals are typically allowed with a 14-day notice period. Some restrictions may apply depending on active loans.

How is interest calculated on loans?

Interest is calculated using the simple interest formula: (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100. Monthly payments are divided equally over the loan term.
