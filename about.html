About CoopSave

A Cooperative Information Management System (CIMS) is a digital platform designed to digitize, connect, and streamline cooperative operations. It empowers cooperative members and administrators by automating workflows, enhancing financial transparency, and simplifying essential management and regulatory processes.

Our Statements

Our Mission

To empower cooperative movements by providing an intuitive, integrated digital platform that promotes transparency, drives operational efficiency, and fosters inclusive economic growth for all members.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable, self-reliant, and highly productive cooperative sector founded on strong principles of collaboration, equitable empowerment, and modern digital innovation.

Our Values

transparency, security, efficiency, and inclusivity—guide every aspect of our platform to build lasting cooperative trust. We safeguard member data with high-level encryption while eliminating slow, manual paperwork through streamlined, real-time digital workflows.
