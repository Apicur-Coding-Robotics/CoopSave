About CoopSave

CoopSave is designed to help small savings groups and SACCOs (Savings and Credit Cooperative Organizations) manage their operations efficiently. We provide simple tools for member management, loan processing, and savings tracking.

👥 Member Management

Register and manage members with complete profiles and transaction history

💰 Loan Processing

Apply for loans, track applications, and calculate interest automatically

💳 Savings Tracking

Monitor individual savings accounts and track contributions over time

📊 Dashboard Analytics

View real-time statistics and payment status for your cooperative
